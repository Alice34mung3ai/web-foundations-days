About QuickNotes

What is QuickNotes?

QuickNotes is a simple web application that allows you to quickly jot down notes and keep track of your ideas.

It is designed to be fast, easy to use, and accessible from any device.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Click the Text Box. Look for the empty area at the top of your screen that says "Add New Note" or "Type here" and click or tap inside it
  2. Start Typing. Type out whatever idea, task, or thought is on your mind before it disappears
  3. press enter to save
  4. View or Clean Up. Look right below the input box to view your saved notes. If you need to fix a mistake, click "Edit", or click "Delete" to clear it away when you are finished.

Features

  • Add new notes with ease
  • View your existing notes
  • Edit or delete notes as needed
  • Responsive design for mobile and desktop use
